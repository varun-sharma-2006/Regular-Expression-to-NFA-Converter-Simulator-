          Open backend/.env in VS Code or Notepad (not the terminal), fill in these two lines, save, and click "Test connection":

          LLM_PROVIDER=gemini
LLM_API_KEY=paste-your-key-here

          Your key stays on your computer: .env is in .gitignore and is never uploaded to GitHub.
